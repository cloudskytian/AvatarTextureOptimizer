# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Initial open-source NDMF implementation.
- Safety-first static/animation material and texture analysis.
- Quality-controlled island/whole-texture scaling.
- Burst raster masks and candidate-pool shape atlas packing.
- GPU resampling, local MS-SSIM support, atlas rendering, and jump-flood filling.
- Multi-platform output profiles, AAO compatibility, i18n, reports, cancellation, deduplication, and extension APIs.
